a letter from your Babu

Dear didi,

Most families have someone who quietly decides everything. Ours has you — except there has never been anything quiet about it. You pick the direction, you take the weight, and you have never once handed it back when it got heavy.

I've watched you love people the way everyone else only talks about loving people. Showing up. Remembering. Noticing the thing nobody said out loud. You hold on to every single person who ever stood behind you, and you make sure they know it.

And you have never let a kindness go unpaid. Somebody does one small thing for you and you file it away somewhere permanent — then hand it back twice over, years later, long after they've forgotten they ever gave it.

You look after this family, and everyone attached to it, exactly the same whether or not anybody ever says thank you. You never made it conditional on being appreciated. I don't know where you learned that. It is the most impressive thing about you.

People call you strict. I know what it actually is. The world out there isn't soft, so you decided a long time ago that it wasn't going to reach us. Every rule you ever made was a wall around somebody you love.

So happy birthday to the one who leads this house. You have carried more of it than anyone will ever properly thank you for. Today you don't carry anything — today we carry you. 🌄

with everything, always — your Babu ❤️
